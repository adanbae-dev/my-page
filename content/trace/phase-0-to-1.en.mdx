The art-direction proof page that was Phase 0's whole output moved to `/art-direction`,
and the home page was replaced with the golden path.

| | Phase 0 | Phase 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Routes | 1 | 8 |
| JS (gzip) | 168.6 KB | 173.7 KB |
| Budget used | 93% | 96% |

The entire navigation costs 5.1 KB of JS. That leaves 7 KB of headroom,
and Phase 2 will run into it.

The proof page was not deleted. It is the record of why the system looks the way it does,
and that record is itself part of what this site exists to show.
