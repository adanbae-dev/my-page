Weekday mornings, before work, I had set aside time to train. **An injury stopped that.**

## The commute

Books. Three kinds are in the bag right now.

- Cosmos
- Korea National Open University coursework — computer science and data science, both at once
- A certification textbook for big-data analysis

Reading and studying travel in the same bag.
The commute is the one stretch of the day nothing interrupts, so that is where they ended up.

## The empty slot

Nothing has moved into the time the training used to take.
Getting back to where I was before the injury is the plan; what goes in the gap until then is undecided.
