I live in a flat in Gangseo District, in Seoul.

Koreans call this size *gukmin-pyeonghyeong*, which means "the people's size" —
it is the most common flat size in the country. Think of a home with about three rooms.

It sits right below a hillside, so the air is good. The windows stay open most days.

Getting to work and back is not hard either.
That is also why that time became reading time —
if I had to stand the whole way, I could not open a book.
