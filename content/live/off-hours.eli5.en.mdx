On weekday mornings I had decided to exercise before going to work.
Then I got hurt, so right now I cannot.

## On the way to work I read

There are three books in my bag.

- **Cosmos** — a book about space, written for anyone.
- **University coursework** — I study at university while working full time.
  Two subjects at once: computer science and data science.
- **A big-data certification book** — a textbook for an exam about working with data.

Books I read for fun and books I read to study sit in the same bag.
The trip to and from work is the only time all day when nobody interrupts me, so that is where reading happens.

## The gap where exercise used to be is still empty

The plan is to get back to where I was before the injury.
What I do with that time until then, I have not decided.
