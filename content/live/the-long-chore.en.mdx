I read without filtering by kind. There is no particular rule for choosing.

It is hard to explain by what the reading yields. **The act of reading is good, and it is fun.**
That is the whole of why it continues.

## What is on the list now

General reading and exam material sit in the same bag. Cosmos and a big-data
certification textbook are side by side, with university coursework between them.

I have not separated what I want to read from what I have to read.
Separating them is how the second pile stops moving.
