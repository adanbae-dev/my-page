Last Saturday I sat the written exam for the big-data certification.
**I guessed at about a third of it.**

I had not studied much. The exam asked exactly that question.
What the score sheet will tell me is a confirmation of something I already know.

## The plan was the commute, and nothing else

I wrote it down here two weeks ago. Three books in the bag, one of them this
exam, and the commute is the one stretch of the day nothing interrupts — so
that is where the studying happened, and nowhere else.

Three books in one stretch, and I expected enough out of it to pass an exam.
**It does not add up that way.**

## The empty slot is still empty

The same entry recorded one more thing: the morning an injury emptied, and
nothing moved into it.

The exam came after that. The slot is still empty. There was one place a day
to move the studying into, and I did not move it.

## The next sitting

Whether I sit it again depends on the result. What needs deciding is not the
number of hours, but which slot.
