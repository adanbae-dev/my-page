I read books without caring what kind they are. I do not really have a rule for choosing either.

If you ask me what I get out of it, it is hard to answer.
Reading is simply fun on its own. That is the whole reason I keep doing it.

## What I am reading now

My bag has books I read for fun and books for an exam at the same time.
There is Cosmos, a book about space, there is a certification textbook, and university coursework in between.

I have not put "books I want to read" and "books I have to read" in separate places.
When I separate them, the ones I have to read keep getting pushed back.
