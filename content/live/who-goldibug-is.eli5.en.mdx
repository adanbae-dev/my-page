This site was made by "Goldibug's father."

Goldibug is the **womb-name** of my son, who is four now.
In Korea a womb-name is a name parents pick and use before the baby is born.

The name came from a **birth dream**.
A birth dream is a dream believed to announce that a baby is on the way.
In that dream I was handed gold, and so he became Goldibug.

It does not carry a wish like "grow up precious." What I was handed in the dream simply happened to be gold.

## How much of the day we get

A little in the morning, a little after work.
On weekends I try to be with him all day.

Added up, the weekday time is not much.

## I am the one learning

Raising him, I learn a step at a time.
It turned out not to be a job where the adult only teaches.

So when I decide something, I try to move in the direction where family weighs more.
