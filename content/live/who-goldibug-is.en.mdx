This site was made by Goldibug's father, and Goldibug is the womb-name of a four-year-old son.

The name came from a birth dream. Gold was handed over in the dream, and so the name stuck.
It was not chosen to mean *precious* — what was handed over simply happened to be gold.

## The hours in a day

A little in the morning. A little after work. Weekends, most of the day where possible.

Added up, the weekday hours are not many.

## Which of us is learning

Raising a child, I am learning a step at a time. It is not a job where one side only teaches.

So when something is decided, I try to move in the direction where the family weighs more.
